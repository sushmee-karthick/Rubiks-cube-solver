How to read moves
RTurn the right face clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it. R'Turn the right face anticlockwise. R2Turn the right face twice (half turn). U D F B LSame idea for the top (Up), bottom (Down), front, back and left faces.
Uses Kociemba's two-phase algorithm, so solutions are usually 20 to 24 moves. Everything runs in your browser.
